Introduction

Traditional Civic Portals Today

  • Passive Collection: Portals simply store text in a general inbox without instant triage.
  • Routing Bottleneck: Citizens must guess the department, resulting in misfiled tickets and days of delay.
  • Language Barrier: Complex English forms exclude rural and non-English speaking citizens.

The Smart Civic System (SCS) Approach

  • AI Decision Engine: Reads text, photos, and GPS to auto-assign priority and department in $<20\text{ms}$.
  • Inclusive Multilingual Access: Built with voice-to-text in Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, and English.
  • Transparent Governance: Citizens, Officers, and Admins operate on one synchronized database.
Key Innovation: Moving from a passive complaint box to an active, automated civic pipeline that validates truth at both submission and resolution.
💡 Speaker Script: "When a civic problem occurs, citizens often don't know who to complain to. SCS removes the guesswork—citizens speak in their native language, and AI routes the ticket to the exact department in milliseconds."
Slide 4

Problem Statement

The Municipal Gap Today How SCS Solves It
Manual Routing Delay: Complaints sit unattended for days while clerks figure out who is responsible. Instant AI Classification: Classifies text into 10 municipal categories and routes to the officer immediately.
Duplicate Ticket Spam: 5 different residents report the same pothole, creating 5 separate work orders. Spatial Duplicate Guard: Haversine GPS radius ($\le 200\text{m}$) + cosine text similarity merges reports and enables upvoting.
False Closure ("Resolved" Fraud): Tickets are marked completed with zero evidence or inspection. AI Proof-of-Work Verification: Gemini Vision audits Before vs. After photos before allowing ticket closure.
Complex Forms Exclude Citizens: Typing long English paragraphs is difficult for many community members. Multilingual Voice Input: Web Speech API allows voice complaints in Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, and English.
💡 Speaker Script: "The current system wastes municipal funds and citizen trust. We tackle manual delay with NLP, duplicate work orders with GPS radius matching, and fake closures with Before/After AI photo auditing."

Literature Review & Background Study

Research Domain Prior Methodology Studied Engineering Takeaway in SCS
Text Classification Salton & Buckley (1988) — TF-IDF with n-grams [1] Chosen for ultra-low latency ($<20\text{ms}$) and high interpretability without cloud GPU expenses.
Object Detection Redmon et al. (2016) — YOLO Architecture [2] Accurate for local bounding boxes; paired with Gemini Multimodal Vision API for zero-GPU cloud deployment.
Explainable AI Ribeiro et al. (2016) — LIME / XAI Principles [3] Trust needs reasons: displays confidence score and influential keywords to the citizen in real time.
Multimodal Vision Google Gemini Multimodal Models (2023) [4] Enables Before vs. After repair verification and catches fake/synthetic wallpaper uploads.
💡 Speaker Script: "Our literature review helped us choose fast, explainable TF-IDF for text classification and Gemini multimodal vision for Before/After repair checks, eliminating the need for expensive local server GPUs."

Data Gathered / Data Used

1. Text Modality

• Free-text complaint description (typed or voice-dictated).
• Extracted n-gram keywords, predicted category, confidence level.
• Preprocessed via stopword filtering and token normalization.

2. Image Modality

• Citizen "Before" evidence photo (JPG/PNG).
• Officer "After" repair photo with AI verification score.
• Heuristic safety check filtering synthetic/wallpaper images.

3. Geospatial & Time Data

• Device GPS coordinates (lat, lon) and Leaflet map pin.
• Reverse-geocoded street address via OpenStreetMap Nominatim.
• Community center geofence radius coordinates and IST timestamps.

4. Audit Trail & Feedback

• Lifecycle events: `Pending` $\to$ `In Progress` $\to$ `Resolved`.
• Community upvote counts, 1–5 star ratings, and remarks.
• Unique tracking references formatted as `SCS-0001`.

💡 Speaker Script: "We process four types of data: complaint descriptions, photographic evidence, GPS locations, and a complete audit timeline that tracks every ticket until resolution."

Methodology — Flow Chart

Phase 1

Citizen Submission

1. Citizen speaks or types in Telugu/English + adds photo & GPS pin.
2. Real-time NLP extracts keywords & predicts category/priority.
3. Spatial check warns if duplicate exists within 200m.

Phase 2

Department Action

4. Ticket `SCS-XXXX` routed to responsible officer.
5. Officer reviews work order and dispatches repair crew.
6. Officer fixes issue & must upload "After" repair photo.

Phase 3

AI Audit & Verification

7. Gemini Vision compares Before vs. After photos.
8. Match $\ge 60$%: Marked `Resolved`; citizen rates 1-5 stars.
9. Mismatch: Quarantined to Admin Desk.

Core Accountability Rule: If an officer uploads a fake or unrelated photo, the Admin rejects the resolution proof, NOT the citizen's complaint. The ticket returns to `In Progress` for re-inspection.
💡 Speaker Script: "This 3-phase flow shows our closed-loop process. Notice phase 3: if an officer uploads a fake resolution photo, the system rejects the officer's proof and forces re-inspection, protecting citizen trust."

Methodology — 4-Tier Architecture

1. Presentation Tier HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap 5, JavaScript • Web Speech API (Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, English) • Leaflet.js with OpenStreetMap tiles • Dark / Light mode
2. Application Tier Flask (Python) for offline prototyping • Node.js / Express / TypeScript (`server.ts`) for cloud production • Role-Based Access Control (Citizen, Officer, Admin)
3. AI & Spatial Tier TF-IDF n-grams + Cosine Similarity (Explainable NLP, $<20\text{ms}$) • Gemini 1.5 Flash Vision API + OpenCV (Photo verification) • Haversine Geofencing
4. Data Tier SQLite for local offline storage • Supabase PostgreSQL (Managed cloud DB with connection pooling) • Cloudinary / local storage for photos

Slide 14

Challenges Faced & Engineering Solutions

Engineering Challenge How Our Team Solved It
Heavy GPU Demands of YOLO Integrated the cloud Gemini 1.5 Flash Vision API with OpenCV, running seamlessly on lightweight cloud hosting without GPU costs.
Overlapping Departments (e.g. Burst Pipe Damaging Road) Implemented Cross-Department Keyword Detection that prompts the citizen to confirm the primary lead department during submission.
Fake or Unrelated Repair Photos Built an automated Before/After AI match score check. Mismatched or identical photos are quarantined to the Admin Desk.
Duplicate Complaints in Neighborhoods Combined Haversine GPS radius checking ($\le 200\text{m}$) with semantic cosine similarity to warn citizens before filing.
💡 Speaker Script: "During development, we solved real constraints: we replaced heavy local GPUs with Gemini Vision API, resolved multi-department conflicts, and built an automated check against fake repair photos."
