Help Center

Everything you need to know about submitting and tracking complaints.

How to Submit a Complaint

  1. Select the complaint category.
  2. Allow GPS or choose the location on the map.
  3. Describe the issue clearly.
  4. Upload an image (recommended).
  5. Click Submit Complaint.

Complaint Status Guide

🟡 Pending

Your complaint has been received and is waiting for review.

🔵 In Progress

Authorities are currently working on resolving the complaint.

🟢 Resolved

The complaint has been successfully resolved.

🔴 Rejected

The complaint could not be processed. Check the rejection reason in "My Complaints".

Tips for Faster Resolution

  • 📍 Enable GPS while submitting the complaint.
  • 📷 Upload a clear image of the issue.
  • 📝 Provide a detailed description.
  • 🗺 Select the correct location on the map.
  • 🚫 Avoid submitting duplicate complaints.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I submit a complaint?

Go to Submit Complaint, choose a category, select your location, describe the issue, optionally upload an image, and submit.

Why is GPS required?

GPS helps verify that the complaint belongs to your community area.

Can I edit my complaint?

No. If you need to add more information, submit a new complaint.

Why was my complaint rejected?

It may be outside the community area, contain insufficient information, or violate complaint guidelines.
